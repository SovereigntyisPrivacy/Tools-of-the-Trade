# Tools of the Trade (TotT)

An offline-first operational, technical, and tactical reference suite designed for standalone field deployment and local-first data persistence[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).

## Architecture Overview & Operational Modules

Tools of the Trade is organized into two primary divisions: actionable client-side operational utilities and offline technical knowledge archives[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span)[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).

    Tools of the Trade
    ├── Operational Tools
    │   ├── Chronos Hub & Master Calendar  [Master time tracking & epoch persistence]
    │   ├── Fleet Payroll & Shift Tracker  [Crew payouts, hour thresholds, shift logs]
    │   ├── Data Vault & Burner Pad        [Isolated storage & auto-wiping scratchpad]
    │   ├── Appointments & Task Creator    [Operational schedules & daily agendas]
    │   ├── Sub Tracker & Budget Engine    [Recurring overhead & capital ledger]
    │   ├── Optical Comm Link              [Hardware LED Morse transmitter & beacon]
    │   ├── Universal Lens & Omni-Scan     [Barcode/QR reader, OCR & payload generator]
    │   ├── Cipher & Keygen                [Password Forge, v4 UUIDs & Key Vault]
    │   ├── Culinary Engine                [Baker %, brine solver, conversion math]
    │   ├── Mindset & PT Recovery          [Wellness metrics & clinical doctor export]
    │   ├── Omni-Calculator & Asset Ledger [Field calculations & gear inventory]
    │   └── Trauma & CPR                   [Tactical field medical protocols]
    │
    └── Knowledge Archives
        ├── Learning Center & Civics       [Field rights & constitutional protections]
        ├── Survival Field Library         [Wilderness, sheltering & water doctrine]
        ├── Botany & Wild Foraging         [Flora identification & processing logs]
        ├── Firearms & Armory Database     [Ballistic references & maintenance rules]

        ├── Pharmacology Database          [Compound references & contraindications]
        └── AI Visual Scanner (BYOK)       [Local-first computer vision processing]

### 1. Hardware & Field Utilities
* **Optical Comm Link:** Directly controls the device's physical camera LED array to transmit optical Morse code transmissions[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span). Includes words-per-minute (WPM) speed modulation, continuous SOS rescue cycling, and real-time payload encoding[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span)[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).
* **Universal Lens (Omni-Scan):** Hardware-accelerated camera and gallery scanner handling 1D/2D barcodes, QR codes, and Deep Text Extraction (OCR)[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span). Generates offline payloads for Wi-Fi networks, crypto transfer requests, vCards, GPS coordinates, SMS, and barcodes[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span).
* **Cipher & Key Vault:** Non-networked cryptographic utility offering pseudo-random key generation with granular character-set entropy, v4 UUID fabrication for mesh hardware, and an isolated local vault[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span)[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span)[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span).

### 2. Operational & Workplace Engines
* **Chronos Hub & Workforce Management:** Client-side operational timecards, fleet payroll distribution models, and shift tracking with epoch timestamp verification[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span)[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span).
* **Culinary Engine:** Commercial baking hydration (Baker's Percentages), equilibrium brining calculations, volume-to-mass conversions using density coefficients, and an interactive recipe builder supporting structured metric rows and camera OCR text imports[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span)[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span).
* **Mindset & Physical Recovery Tracker:** Tracks physical fatigue, stress indexes, focus levels, and physical therapy recovery logs[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span)[span_18](start_span)[span_18](end_span). Features a native Android Share Intent formatting daily records into clinical terminology for medical providers[span_19](start_span)[span_19](end_span)[span_20](start_span)[span_20](end_span).
